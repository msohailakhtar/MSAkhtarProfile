About

Mathematics → Learning → Decision

I'm a mathematics- and AI-focused university academic, currently pursuing a PhD in Artificial Intelligence & Autonomous Systems at the National University of Sciences & Technology (NUST), Islamabad. My work combines a strong foundation in applied mathematics, numerical analysis and mathematical modeling with practical, Python-based expertise in machine learning, data analytics and business intelligence.

I'm a founding member and initiator of PMAI Lab — an academic initiative bringing Python, Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence together into one collaborative teaching and research environment for faculty and students.

My teaching record spans over six years of undergraduate instruction across two Pakistani universities, with experience in outcome-based education, curriculum delivery and academic coordination.
