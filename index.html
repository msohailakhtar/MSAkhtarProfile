ABOUT

Academic Profile

Muhammad Sohail Akhtar is an academic and researcher whose foundation is in Applied Mathematics and whose current research direction is Artificial Intelligence and Autonomous Systems.

His academic interests focus on bringing mathematical reasoning, optimization and computational methods into modern Artificial Intelligence, particularly for learning and decision-making under uncertainty.

He is currently pursuing a PhD in AI & Autonomous Systems at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Islamabad , with particular interest in mathematically grounded Reinforcement Learning and autonomous decision-making.

ACADEMIC INITIATIVE

PMAI Lab

Founding Member & Initiator

Python, Mathematics & Artificial Intelligence Lab

PMAI Lab is an academic initiative developed around the idea of bringing Mathematics, Python and Artificial Intelligence together in one collaborative learning and research environment.

The vision is to create a shared academic platform where faculty members and students can access structured courses, mathematical foundations, programming resources, AI learning materials, research notes, projects and open educational resources.

The initiative is designed as a collaborative environment for faculty and students interested in developing strong foundations in mathematics, programming and AI while encouraging research-oriented learning and open-source academic development.
