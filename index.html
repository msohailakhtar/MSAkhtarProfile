01 · About

Mathematics as a language for intelligent systems.

My academic work combines mathematical thinking with modern computational methods to understand, model, and build intelligent systems.

I am a mathematics educator and PhD Scholar in AI & Autonomous Systems at NUST, with a research direction focused on mathematically grounded approaches to machine learning and autonomous decision-making.

My academic journey began with mathematics and gradually expanded toward computational mathematics, machine learning, data science, optimization, and artificial intelligence.

I am particularly interested in the connection between mathematical structure and learning algorithms — especially where mathematical reasoning can make intelligent systems more interpretable, reliable, and efficient.
