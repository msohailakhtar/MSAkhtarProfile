01 • ABOUT

Academic & Research Profile

Muhammad Sohail Akhtar is an academic and researcher with a foundation in Applied Mathematics and a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Reinforcement Learning.

His work focuses on connecting mathematical foundations with modern Artificial Intelligence to develop intelligent systems capable of learning, reasoning and making reliable decisions in complex and uncertain environments.

He is pursuing a PhD in AI & Autonomous Systems at NUST and is particularly interested in mathematically grounded Reinforcement Learning, optimization and autonomous decision-making.
